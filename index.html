About Benjamin Harte

Benjamin Harte, born Benjamin Okafor on December 28, 2006, is a Nigerian pop and R&B singer-songwriter from Delta State, Nigeria. Blending contemporary pop, R&B, and acoustic influences, his music draws from love, life experiences, faith, vulnerability, and the emotions that shape our lives.

Benjamin’s passion for music began at 16, when he started singing at school. With limited access to professional recording equipment, he found another way to pursue his dream. After discovering BandLab, he began writing, recording, and creating music using his phone. What started with simple tools became the foundation of his journey as an artist.

As a singer-songwriter, Benjamin Harte aims to create honest and relatable music that connects with listeners emotionally. His songs explore love, personal experiences, faith, hope, and the realities of growing into who you are. Still at the beginning of his journey, Benjamin is committed to developing his sound, improving his craft, and reaching audiences beyond Nigeria. His dream is to take his music to a global audience, collaborate with talented artists and producers, and eventually work with a record label that believes in his vision. For Benjamin Harte, this is only the beginning.
